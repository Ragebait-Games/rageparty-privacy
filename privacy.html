Rage Party – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 October 2026

Rage Party is a two-player party game. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and what choices you have. This version of the game has no accounts, no sign-up and no online multiplayer, so we collect and keep almost nothing.

In one paragraph

Your progress, your funny guest name and your settings stay on your own device. We do not run a server that stores anything about you. The game is free and shows ads: an occasional full-screen ad between races and an optional video you choose to watch for a cosmetic reward (never during a race). Those ads are served by Google, which may use device identifiers for that purpose. See "Advertising" below.

Who we are

The game is provided by Ragebait Games. Questions or requests: open an issue at https://github.com/Ragebait-Games/rageparty-privacy/issues.

We do not collect your real name, e-mail, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos or microphone. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes everything above.

Advertising

  • The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob: an occasional full-screen ad between races, and an optional "watch a video for a hat" offer on the results page that you may take or ignore. There are no banner ads. Ads never appear during a race.
  • To serve ads Google may collect and use your advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device information and how you interact with ads. We do not receive that data.
  • Where required by law (for example in the EU, EEA and UK) you are asked for consent in the game, and you can change that choice later in your device's ad settings.
  • You can limit ad personalisation or reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • More about how Google uses data in ads: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • The pop-ups you have to close inside a level (the "tiny X") are part of the game, not real ads. They are never linked to anything outside the game.

Computer opponents

Single-player games are against a computer-controlled opponent that runs on your device.

Sharing

We do not sell your information and we do not share it, except that Google receives advertising data as described above. We may disclose information if the law requires it, but we hold no personal data to disclose.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. It does not ask for personal information and has no chat or online contact between players.

Security

Because nothing is stored on our servers, there is nothing of yours for us to lose. Ad data is handled by Google under its own privacy policy.

Device backups

Your phone's own backup feature (for example Android backup to your Google account) may include the game's saved progress and settings. That is controlled by your device settings and the platform provider's policy.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete everything the game stores by clearing its data in your device settings or uninstalling it.
  • Change your guest name in the profile page at any time.
  • Control ads with your device's ad settings (see "Advertising").

Changes

If we add features that change what we collect (for example online play with friends), we will update this page and its date, and tell players in the game before the new features collect anything.

Contact

Ragebait Games – open an issue at https://github.com/Ragebait-Games/rageparty-privacy/issues. We read every one.
