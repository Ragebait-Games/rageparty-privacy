Rage Party – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 October 2026

Rage Party is a two-player party game. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and what choices you have. This version of the game has no accounts of its own, no sign-up and no online multiplayer. You may optionally sign in with Google Play Games to back up your progress, and otherwise we collect and keep almost nothing.

In one paragraph

Your progress, your funny guest name and your settings stay on your own device. If you sign in with Google Play Games, your progress is also saved in your Google account so you do not lose it on a new phone. We do not run a server that stores anything about you. The game is free and shows no ads in this version. See "Advertising" below.

Who we are

The game is provided by Ragebait Games. Questions or requests: open an issue at https://github.com/Ragebait-Games/rageparty-privacy/issues.

We do not collect your real name, e-mail, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos or microphone. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes everything above.

Advertising

  • This version of the game shows no ads and does not collect or use your advertising ID.
  • If we add ads in a later update, we will update this page first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
  • The pop-ups you have to close inside a level (the "tiny X") are part of the game, not real ads. They are never linked to anything outside the game.

Google Play Games (optional)

  • On Android the game may sign you in with Google Play Games, usually automatically if you already use it on the phone. You can also tap the cloud button on the profile page.
  • If you are signed in, the game writes a small saved game (your progress) to your Google account and reads it back on other devices. Google handles that data under the Google Privacy Policy and Play Games terms. We receive nothing.
  • If you do not sign in, nothing is backed up and everything stays on this device as described above. You can delete saved games in the Play Games app (Settings → Delete Play Games account and data).

Computer opponents

Single-player games are against a computer-controlled opponent that runs on your device.

Sharing

We do not sell your information and we do not share it, except that, if you sign in with Google Play Games, your saved game as described above. We may disclose information if the law requires it, but we hold no personal data to disclose.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. It does not ask for personal information and has no chat or online contact between players.

Security

Because nothing is stored on our servers, there is nothing of yours for us to lose.

Device backups

Your phone's own backup feature (for example Android backup to your Google account) may include the game's saved progress and settings. That is controlled by your device settings and the platform provider's policy.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete everything the game stores by clearing its data in your device settings or uninstalling it. A Play Games saved game is removed in the Play Games app, not by uninstalling.
  • Change your guest name in the profile page at any time.

Changes

If we add features that change what we collect (for example online play with friends), we will update this page and its date, and tell players in the game before the new features collect anything.

Contact

Ragebait Games – open an issue at https://github.com/Ragebait-Games/rageparty-privacy/issues. We read every one.
